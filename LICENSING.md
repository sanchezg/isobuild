## Corresponding source for the distributed version

Every build includes `IsoBuild-Source.zip`, generated automatically from that
project during the build. It contains sources, tests, language resources,
configuration, the Xcode project, scripts, instructions, and legal files.
It can be saved or shared from About IsoBuild.

Redistributors must provide their own updated corresponding source under
section 6, not merely source for an earlier version. Their distribution method
must also preserve all other applicable GPLv3 conditions.
